Overview

What is the Hornbill Aviation membership?

Hornbill Aviation's membership is a $59/month subscription for pilots training or renting at Reno–Tahoe. Members pay $159/hr wet for our PA28 fleet (billed as tach time × 1.3). That saves $20/hr over the non-member rate. There is no contract, and it includes priority scheduling, unlimited ground school, unlimited access to our FAA-certified CR-12 AATD, and cross-country rental eligibility. See the Instrument Rating page for details on AATD time creditable toward a rating.

FAQ

Membership questions

What is included in the $59/month membership?

The membership lowers the PA28 wet rate to $159/hr, gives you priority scheduling, unlimited ground school access, unlimited access to our FAA-certified CR-12 AATD, a 12-hour booking cancellation window, and cross-country rental eligibility. It does not include instructor time or the aircraft rental hours themselves. See the Instrument Rating page for how AATD hours count toward a rating.

How much do I save per flight hour?

Members pay $159/hr wet for the PA28 fleet. Non-members pay $179/hr. That is a $20/hr savings, so the membership pays for itself at just under three flight hours per month.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No. The membership is month to month. You can cancel anytime with no cancellation fee.

Can non-members still rent aircraft?

Yes. Non-members can rent the same PA28 fleet at the non-member wet rate of $179/hr. The membership is simply a way to save if you fly regularly.

Does the membership include instructor fees?

No. Flight and ground instruction are billed separately from aircraft rental. See the Fleet & Pricing page for current CFI rates.

Can I use the member rate for cross-country rentals?

Yes. The member rate applies to all PA28 rental flights, including cross-country trips. IFR-equipped aircraft are available for instrument cross-countries.

How do I cancel or change my membership?

You can manage or cancel your membership through your account, or contact us by phone, text, or email. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.
